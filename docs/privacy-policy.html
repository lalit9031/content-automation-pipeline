Privacy Policy

Content Automation Pipeline | Effective date: May 26, 2026

Purpose

Content Automation Pipeline is a personal content-creation application. It generates draft captions and images and may, after authorization, help its owner publish content to the owner's LinkedIn profile.

Information Used

The application may process:

  • Content prompts, generated captions, hashtags, and generated images.
  • LinkedIn authorization data required to publish posts requested by the account owner.
  • Basic technical run records used to confirm whether content was prepared or published.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to generate content, prepare posts for review, and publish content to an authorized LinkedIn profile when that publishing feature is enabled by the owner.

What The Application Does Not Do

  • It does not sell personal information.
  • It does not send automated messages or connection requests.
  • It does not access LinkedIn messages or connections for this publishing workflow.
  • It does not publish to LinkedIn unless authorized publishing is enabled.

Third-Party Services

The application may use OpenAI for content generation, Google Cloud Vertex AI for generated images, and LinkedIn for authorized post publishing. Use of those services is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Data Control And Revocation

The account owner can disable publishing in the application configuration and can revoke LinkedIn authorization through LinkedIn account or application settings. Local generated files and credentials can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Contact

Questions about this application or this privacy policy can be raised through the project repository: github.com/lalit9031/content-automation-pipeline.
